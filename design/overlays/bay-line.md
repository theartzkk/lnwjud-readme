# BAY LINE / MINI App Experience Overlay

Authority: `design/DESIGN.md` and `design/UX-ACCEPTANCE.md`.

## Product intent
Conversation-first, mobile-first school actions inside LINE. The next action and role boundary must be obvious without requiring the web control panel.

## Required contracts
- Rich Menu destinations are role-correct and deterministic.
- Teacher, cooperative and admin menu projections do not expose irrelevant primary actions.
- Substitute-teaching flow preserves preview, confirm, transaction, notification and audit semantics.
- A denied action explains the available next step instead of ending at a dead end.
- MINI App and LIFF surfaces respect safe areas, keyboard, visual viewport and LINE WebView constraints.
- Loading, retry and error states use concise Thai-first user language rather than provider terminology.
- Return, back and close behavior preserve the LINE host context and unfinished work where promised.
