# BAY Hub Overlay

BAY Hub is the ecosystem/status/control projection expression of KRUART.

- Feel: concise, truthful, operational overview.
- Status/release labels must describe real evidence; never imply deployed/healthy from source state alone.
- Present systems as one ecosystem without creating duplicate product/data authorities.
- Reuse AWH/BAY canonical project identity and existing registry contracts.
- Health, release and integration states are information-first and may be denser than public surfaces.
- Decorative assets are secondary to provenance, revision and action clarity.
- Computer Lab and other projections remain references to their canonical runtime/source, not copied authorities.
