# BAY LearnLab Overlay

LearnLab is the classroom/student-friendly expression of KRUART.

- Feel: energetic enough for primary learners, calm enough for classroom control.
- Student UI uses clear large actions, limited simultaneous choices and age-appropriate language.
- Teacher Workspace stays operational and compact; projector views prioritize distance readability.
- Preserve shared-PC, offline-first, immutable runtime and classroom resilience contracts.
- BAY Host Contract is the boundary to school identity/schedule/roster authority.
- Product source must not reintroduce direct Bay\Core dependencies or BAY master-table queries.
- Team/game visuals may be playful, but status cannot be color-only and accessibility/focus remain required.
- 28-student / six-station classroom reality is a core usability constraint.
