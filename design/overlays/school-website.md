# School Website Overlay

The School Website is the public, warm and trustworthy expression of KRUART.

- Feel: approachable school/community presence, not an authenticated admin shell.
- Public navigation is simple, mobile-friendly and content-first.
- Do not inherit signed-in AWH bottom navigation or Owner controls.
- Parent Connect entry may link into the canonical connected service; do not recreate its identity/data authority.
- School name/logo/contact identity must remain exact.
- News, staff, school activity and parent information use semantic approved assets and readable HTML copy.
- Avoid decorative density that competes with public information.
- Privacy, external-link and content QA remain release gates.

## Signature school editorial expression

- Lead with the **real school and its community**, not a generic institutional template. Use approved school logos, verified name, real campus/activity photographs and genuine published news. Do not fill the page with invented quotes, statistics, staff, pupils or events.
- Establish a clear hierarchy between one current school story or parent-facing task, recent news, school identity and useful public links. Vary image/text balance with authentic editorial pacing rather than repeating the same grid for every content type.
- Preserve the KRUART blue/orange and Thai type baseline while expressing a warm school personality through real imagery, whitespace, accessible cropping and legible information.
- Motion should support story changes and navigation without fighting scrolling or hiding content. Auto-advancing elements must remain readable and respect reduced motion.
- All changes reuse the existing website content/publishing/privacy paths; no parallel CMS, identity layer or presentation stylesheet owner.
