# BAY Assessment Experience Overlay

Authority: `design/DESIGN.md` and `design/UX-ACCEPTANCE.md`.

## Product intent
High-trust teacher assessment workflow. Accuracy, legibility, recovery and print fidelity outrank decorative density.

## Required contracts
- Teacher/admin identity and permission presentation must be explicit; no ambiguous role chrome.
- Answer-key edits, submission state, scanner review and logout must provide immediate deterministic feedback.
- Scanner results must never be visually presented as certain when confidence requires review.
- Print Center must expose ready/not-ready state with an actionable next step.
- Thai text, subject names and student names must not clip or substitute incorrectly.
- Answer sheets and official exam documents are print-fidelity surfaces; geometry changes require dedicated evidence.
- Mobile form focus, paste, IME, keyboard and primary actions must remain visible/recoverable.
